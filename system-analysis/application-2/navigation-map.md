# CiiHive Navigation Map

The hierarchy below groups the 177 observed menu-route targets. Screen names
are taken from the visible menu; multiple operations may share a label.

```text
Designer
├── Module Designer
├── Form Designer
├── Submodule Designer
├── Workflow Designer
├── Report Designer
└── Menu Designer

CRM
├── Registration: Individual Customer, Business Customer
├── Freeze / Unfreeze: Freez Accounts, Unfreeze Account, Freeze Account
├── Block / Unblock
├── Follow Up
├── Modification: Individual Customer, Business Customer
├── Approval: Individual/Business Customer, Freeze/Unfreeze Account, Block/UnBlock
├── Blacklist: Customer Blacklisting, Customer Whitelist, Unclose Customer
├── Credit Scoring: Client Evaluation
└── Advanced Operations: Mark Close Application

Loan
├── Application: Individual Loan, Group Loan
├── Collaterals, Credit Committee, Register Groups, Mocked Loan Schedule
├── Restructure, Charges Waiver, Monthly Targets
├── Modification / Approval: Individual Loan, Group Loan, Loan Account Rate,
│   Loan Charges Waiver
├── Advanced Operations: Closure, Write Off, Reschedule, Edit Schedule,
│   Appeal, Early Settlement, Cheques, Promissory Note, Marking Status,
│   Follow Up, Installment Deferment, Bulk FollowUp
└── Loan Accounting / Authorization / Cancellation

Deposit and Term Deposit
├── Account Open / Modify / Approve (Individual and Business)
├── Deposit rate, charge exemption, audit, closure, profit posting
├── Cash deposit / withdrawal, instruments, cheques, transaction imports
├── TDR registration, issuance, encashment, early encashment
└── Deposit accounting and SOC

General Ledger / User Batch
├── Batch open, replenishment, cash to vault, close, verification
├── GL-GL, Excel/bulk uploads, creation, modification, authorization, reversal
└── COA / GL Matrix

Security / Configuration / Administration
├── Users, roles, activity allocation, device/geographic settings
├── Branch/company/product/functionality configuration
├── Replication, blacklisting, daily/period close and year-end operations
├── Notification/message/reminder setup
└── Bank Setup, Company Workflow, Instrument Approval, Reversal
```

See the [module inventory](./module-inventory.md) for all visible labels and
per-group route counts. Routes were navigated but their forms were not
submitted.
